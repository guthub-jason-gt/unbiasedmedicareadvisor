Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I pay when Medicare agents are free?

"Free" Medicare agents are paid by insurance companies — which means they're incentivized to steer you toward plans that pay THEM the highest commission, not plans that are best for YOU. When you pay us directly, our only loyalty is to you. We research every option without bias and recommend what actually fits your situation.

How does the consultation work?

After you purchase a package, we'll send you a short questionnaire (or gather the info by phone for White Glove). We'll research the plans available in your area, analyze your medications and doctors, and create a detailed PDF report with our recommendations. You'll have email (and phone, depending on package) access to ask questions and get clarification.

What exactly do I get for my money?

You get a comprehensive PDF report that compares your options and explains exactly which plan we recommend — and why. You also get follow-up support via email (and phone for larger packages) to answer any questions. Most importantly, you get peace of mind knowing your Medicare decision was made with YOUR best interest in mind, not an insurance company's.

Can you help my parents?

Absolutely. Many clients hire us to help their aging parents navigate Medicare. The White Glove package is especially popular for this — we handle everything by phone, so your parent doesn't need to fill out any forms or use a computer.

I'm turning 65 soon. When should I start?

Ideally, contact us 2-3 months before your 65th birthday. This gives us time to research your options before your Medicare enrollment period begins. But even if you're already on Medicare and want a second opinion on your coverage, we can help any time of year.
