What Is a Fee-Only Medicare Advisor?

A fee-only Medicare advisor is an independent consultant who charges you directly for their expertise and receives zero commissions, kickbacks, or compensation from insurance companies. Unlike commission-based agents who get paid when you enroll in certain plans, a fee-only advisor's income comes entirely from the consultation fee you pay.

This simple difference changes everything. When you work with a fee-only Medicare advisor, there's no hidden agenda. No wondering if the plan being recommended is actually best for you or just the one that pays the highest commission. Your advisor's only incentive is to find the Medicare solution that truly fits your healthcare needs and budget.

A fee-only Medicare consultant provides the same comprehensive guidance as commission-based agents—plan comparisons, enrollment help, and ongoing support—but without the conflict of interest that comes from insurance company payments.

The Problem with Commission-Based Medicare Advisors

Most Medicare agents and brokers advertise their services as "free." But here's what they don't tell you: they earn commissions from insurance companies every time you enroll in a plan. This creates serious conflicts of interest:

  • They may only show you plans that pay commissions. Some excellent Medicare options (like certain Medigap plans) may be overlooked if they don't compensate the agent.
  • Higher-commission plans get pushed harder. Medicare Advantage plans often pay agents $600+ per enrollment, while Medigap commissions can be lower. Guess which gets recommended more?
  • Annual plan switching benefits the agent. Each time you switch plans, agents can earn new commissions—even if staying put would be better for you.
  • Your best interests take a back seat. When someone is paid by insurance companies, their loyalty is split. You deserve an advisor whose only loyalty is to you.

How Fee-Only Advice Is Different

When you choose a non-commission Medicare advisor, you get guidance that's 100% focused on your needs:

  • Every option on the table. We compare Medicare Supplement plans, Medicare Advantage plans, and Part D options from ALL carriers—not just those that pay commissions.
  • Honest recommendations. If staying with Original Medicare and a Medigap plan is best for you, that's what we'll recommend—even if it means less money for us.
  • No pressure sales tactics. We're not trying to hit enrollment quotas or earn bonuses. Take the time you need to make the right decision.
  • Transparent relationship. You know exactly what you're paying and why. No hidden agendas, no wondering about ulterior motives.
  • Long-term thinking. We recommend plans that will serve you well for years—not plans that benefit from annual switching.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a fee-only Medicare advisor cost?

Fee-only Medicare advisors typically charge a flat consultation fee rather than earning commissions from insurance companies. Fees vary by advisor and scope of service, but many charge between $150-$500 for a comprehensive Medicare consultation. This fee covers a thorough analysis of your healthcare needs, comparison of all available options, and personalized written recommendations. Contact us for our current rates.

How is fee-only different from free Medicare help?

When Medicare help is "free," the advisor is typically being paid commissions by insurance companies when you enroll in their plans. This creates a potential conflict of interest—they may only show you plans that pay them commissions, or steer you toward higher-commission options. A fee-only Medicare advisor is paid directly by you, which means our only incentive is to find the best plan for YOUR situation, not the plan that pays us the most.

Do fee-only advisors work with all insurance companies?

Yes! Because we don't earn commissions, we have no reason to favor one insurance company over another. We can objectively compare plans from ALL carriers available in your area—including Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, Medicare Advantage plans, and Part D prescription drug plans. We'll recommend whichever option truly fits your healthcare needs and budget, regardless of the insurance company.

Is fee-only Medicare advice worth it?

For many people, absolutely. Choosing the wrong Medicare plan can cost you thousands of dollars per year in unnecessary premiums, out-of-pocket costs, or inadequate coverage. A fee-only advisor's consultation fee is often paid back many times over through savings from choosing the right plan. Plus, you get peace of mind knowing your advisor's recommendations are 100% based on your best interests, not hidden financial incentives.
