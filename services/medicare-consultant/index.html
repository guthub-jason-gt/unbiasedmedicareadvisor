Medicare Consulting Services

Professional Medicare consulting services to help you navigate enrollment, compare plans, and make confident coverage decisions—with zero commissions or sales pressure.

What Are Medicare Consulting Services?

Medicare consulting services provide professional guidance to help you navigate the complex world of Medicare. Unlike insurance agents who earn commissions by selling specific plans, Medicare consulting services offer objective, unbiased advice focused entirely on your needs—not on which plan pays the highest commission.

Whether you're turning 65, leaving employer coverage, reviewing your current plan during Annual Enrollment, or simply feeling overwhelmed by your options, Medicare consulting services can help you make confident, informed decisions about your healthcare coverage.

Our Medicare consulting services are designed for people who want:

  • Unbiased plan recommendations with no hidden agendas
  • Clear explanations of complex Medicare rules and options
  • Personalized analysis based on their specific health needs and budget
  • Ongoing support and guidance throughout enrollment
  • A trusted advisor who works for them—not the insurance companies

As a fee-only Medicare advisor, we charge a transparent fee for our Medicare consulting services. This means you get advice you can trust, without wondering if we're steering you toward a plan that benefits us more than you.

Medicare Consulting Services vs. Free SHIP Counseling

You may have heard about SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program), which offers free Medicare counseling. Both options can help, but there are important differences to consider when deciding which Medicare consulting services are right for you.

Feature Our Medicare Consulting Services Free SHIP Counseling
Availability Available year-round, flexible scheduling Often limited hours, long wait times during enrollment periods
Personalization Deep dive into your specific situation, medications, doctors, and budget General information and guidance
Depth of Analysis Comprehensive plan comparison with detailed cost projections Overview of options and how to research them
Follow-up Support Ongoing support via phone and email for weeks after consultation Typically limited to the initial session
Wait Times Appointments typically within 1-3 days Can be weeks during peak enrollment season
Cost $250-$1,000 depending on service level Free

SHIP counselors are volunteers who do important work. For quick questions, they're a great resource. For complex situations requiring detailed analysis, professional Medicare consulting services provide more comprehensive support.

Frequently Asked Questions About Medicare Consulting Services

How much do Medicare consulting services cost?
Our fee-only Medicare consulting services typically range from $250-$1,000 depending on the complexity of your situation. Unlike commission-based agents, we charge a transparent fee and have no financial incentive to recommend one plan over another.
What's the difference between a Medicare consultant and a Medicare agent?
A Medicare consultant provides objective guidance and charges you directly for advice. A Medicare agent sells insurance plans and earns commissions from insurance companies. Our fee-only approach means our recommendations are based solely on what's best for you—not on which plan pays us more.
When should I use Medicare consulting services?
Consider Medicare consulting services when you're turning 65, leaving employer coverage, unhappy with your current plan, taking expensive medications, or simply overwhelmed by the options. We're especially helpful during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 - December 7).
Do you sell Medicare plans?
No. We provide Medicare consulting services only. We'll research and recommend the best options for your situation, but you'll enroll directly with the insurance company or through Medicare.gov. This ensures our advice is 100% unbiased.
Can you help me if I live outside Florida?
Yes! Our Medicare consulting services are available nationwide. We work with clients in all 50 states via phone and video consultations.
