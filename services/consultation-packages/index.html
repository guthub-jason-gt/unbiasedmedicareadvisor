Full Medicare Analysis

$500
Who we built this for If you already have Medicare, we compare the choices in front of you: Medicare Supplement, also called Medigap, Medicare Advantage, and Part D prescription drug plans. This is not a one time decision. Many clients come back each autumn because plans can change on January 1. Doctors, drugs, benefits, and costs can all be different.

What we include

  • Analysis of every Medigap plan available in your state
  • Analysis of all Medicare Advantage plans available in your county
  • Your medicines checked against every Part D drug plan available to you
  • Checking that your own doctors and pharmacies are covered by the plans being compared
  • Reviewing all of your Medicare deadline dates together by telephone
  • A written report delivered as a PDF file by email
  • Two hours of telephone advisory
  • Six rounds of email follow up across one month

If you do not have Medicare yet, you need the White Glove package instead.

If you have Medicare plus another kind of coverage, please look at our White Glove page before you choose this package.

White Glove Service

$1,000
Who we built this for White Glove includes everything in the Full Medicare Analysis package, plus more help through the whole Medicare setup process. It is the package you need if you are not yet on Medicare, or if your Medicare has to work alongside another system of coverage, such as federal retirement.

What we include

  • Everything in our Full Medicare Analysis package
  • We guide you through getting your Medicare number and Parts A and B
  • We work through your IRMAA* bracket, Medicare's income surcharge, and any path to appeal it
  • We explain how Medicare fits with federal or postal retirement coverage, Railroad Retirement, TRICARE, VA care, or a job plan, including which system pays first, what each leaves unpaid, and where they overlap
  • Unlimited consultations, until we both agree this year's analysis is complete
* IRMAA means Income Related Monthly Adjustment Amount. It is an extra charge Medicare adds to Part B and Part D premiums for higher income households.
